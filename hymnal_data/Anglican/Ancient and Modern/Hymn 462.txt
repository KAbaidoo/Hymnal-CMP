 HYMN 462 
 
 Herald, in the wilderness 
 Breaking up the road, 
 Sinking mountains, raising plains,
 For the path of GOD; 
  
 
 Prophet, to the multitudes 
 Calling to repent, 
 In the way of righteousness 
 Unto Israel sent; 
  
 
 Messenger, GOD’s chosen one 
 Fore most to proclaim, 
 Proffer’d titles, passing by,
 Pointing to the Lamb; 
  
 
 Captive, for the world of truth
 Boldly witnessing; 
 Then in Herod’s dungeon-cave
 Faint and languishing; 
  
 
 Martyr, sacrifice to sin 
 At that feast of shame; 
 As his life foreshow’d the LORD,
 In his death the same
  
 
 Holy JESUS, when He hear, 
 Went apart to pray: 
 Thus may we our lesson take 
 From His Saint to-day.
