 HYMN 68 
 The Innocents’ Day 
 
 Sweet flow’ rets on the martyr band, 
 So early pluck’d by cruel hand; 
 Like rosebuds by a tempest torn, 
 As breaks the light o! summer morn; 
  
 First victims offer’d for the LORD, 
 Ye little knew your high reward, 
 As, at the very altar, gay 
 With palms and crowns ye seem’d to play 
  
 
 Ah! What avail'd King Herod’s wrath? 
 He could not stay your avicur’ path: 
 The Child he sought alone went free; 
 That child is King eternally. 
  
 
 O LORD, the Virgin-born, to Thee 
 Praise, honour, might, and glory be, 
 Who with the FATHER we adore 
 And HOLY GHOST for evermore.
